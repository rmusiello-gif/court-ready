COACHING KEYS
THE ESSENTIALS
What separates players who get faster from those who just train harder.
01
The drop step is a technique, not fitness
Your backward movement weakness is the pivot pattern — not your conditioning. Walk it slowly first. Speed comes after the pattern is clean. Adding speed before that just ingrains the wrong movement.
02
Rest fully between agility sets
90–120 seconds between every agility rep. Fatigued agility training becomes cardio — you'll get slower, not faster. When in doubt, rest more.
03
Split step timing is everything
Land at the EXACT moment your opponent's racket contacts the ball. Not before (you'll commit too early), not after (you'll be flat). This is the single biggest fix for flat-footedness.
04
Read the shoulder, not the ball
Your opponent's shoulder rotation tells you where the ball is going 0.2–0.3 seconds earlier than watching the racket or ball. Train yourself to scan their shoulders during the swing.
05
5 min of hip + ankle mobility every morning
90/90 hip switch + ankle wall mobilization. Every day, even rest days. This unlocks the hip range your drop step needs. You'll feel the difference in 2 weeks.
06
Progress in 3-week blocks
Weeks 1–2: learn the pattern at 50–70% speed. Week 3: full intensity. Week 4: deload at 50% volume. Adaptation happens in the deload — don't skip it.
